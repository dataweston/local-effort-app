## Send decision system

Score each prospect 0–2 on four observable dimensions: (1) match to recurring beat; (2) timing of a current storyline; (3) verified reporting materials we can provide; (4) distribution value *if the reporter elects to cover it* (prominence and relevance of readership, not speculative private social motivations). A score above 6 earns a single individualized pitch. Score 4–6 means cultivate a relationship or gather evidence first. Scores below 4 get no outreach. Re-evaluate monthly and log feedback without inferring intent from email opens.

### Contact routing

- Personal journalist pitches should ordinarily be plain text and uniquely addressed. Do not automatically enroll reporters into Brevo's bulk-marketing contact lists or assume prior imported marketing records equal permission to send campaigns. For a person who opted in to press announcements, an appropriate Brevo list could be used once list integrity is checked.
- Never send more than one story idea to the same writer simultaneously. Offer first access only if genuine. Disclose any paid participation, sponsorship, or conflicts.
- If someone asks not to be contacted, respect that immediately. If a reporter declines, preserve the relationship and wait for a genuinely distinct reported development.
- All economic assertions require supporting invoices or payroll data. Cooperative and governance claims require current organizing documents. Co-founder and employee views must be checked before attributed quotation.
