# Local Effort — Twin Cities editorial intelligence

Research snapshot: 2026-10-23. **21 contacts** (20 primary + one adjacent); contact email shown only where publicly verified or already stored in Local Effort Brevo. A blank email means *not verified*, not permission to guess. Editorial interests are inferences from observable bylines, not claims about private motivation. Examples are linked for checking. Do not mass-enroll journalists into customer promotional lists. No send is authorized by this file.

## Method and editorial relationship workflow

1. Monthly: check each person's latest 3–5 bylines, beat, employer, and contact method; add dated sources and revise hypotheses rather than guessing.
2. Create an evidence matrix: subject, protagonist, tension, reported numbers, reporting access, current peg, editor-level value; reject pitches without at least two independent elements beyond our own description.
3. Match **one** pitch to each writer based on recent bylines. Contacts can overlap themes without receiving multiple concurrent blasts. Record which prior article directly motivated each approach.
4. Create four *draft-only* Brevo campaigns as a copy library. For earned-media cold pitches, prefer individual, plain-text messages and customized first paragraphs via authenticated professional email, not a promotional Brevo list; Brevo drafts are review artifacts. Keep outlet reporting and paid advertorials distinct.
5. Log date, address provenance, angle, reply, interview, follow-up and publication. Follow up once after 5–7 business days when relevant; stop after decline/opt-out. Treat Brevo opens and clicks as weak signals.
6. Route significant public events to the Sanity `release` content type. `/releases` is CMS-driven, while `api/feeds/releases.rss.js` and atom source must be made to use the same CMS data before claiming syndication works. Historical email templates from Sept 2025 should not be reused as current facts.
7. Before publishing, verify formation, equity structure, operations, employment claims, ownership, venue permissions and availability against documents, and obtain consent for names, photos and employee quotes.
