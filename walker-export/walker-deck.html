Statement

Exhibiting and celebrating Minnesotan excellence for a global audience.

Hospitality at Walker Art Center should center Midwestern people, and Midwestern ingredients, for regional and international audiences, for patrons and visitors, for kids and for grownups, for families and for students. Local Effort proposes an obsessively local restaurant at Walker Art Center.

Local Effort represents local interests through highly curated ingredient selection, culinary and service excellence, and socially progressive labor practice. A collaboration with Local Effort is a commitment to Midwestern values.

From casual to refined formats,
a local-first commitment.

The Opportunity

Our collaboration brings local ingredients
to every visitor experience.

i.
Year-round, world-class greenmarket sets the vibe during the day. Peak-harvest seasonal produce all summer. Get a locally-grown greenhouse cucumber in January.
ii.
Casual breakfast and lunch, with substantial grab-and-go options, for every type of diner. Refined, elevated dinner experiences with classic table service to show off our local culinary prowess.
iii.
Genre-defining meal prep subscription program to feed families all week. Filling a huge gap and growing demand in our community, and celebrating home cooks and Midwestern home cooking.
iv.
Robust on-site events capacity and vision. Supporting Walker's event needs, and showcasing Midwestern hospitality with style and substance.
v.
Tiered pricing for the public, memberships, Walker staff discounts. Meeting communities where they are at.
vi.
Ownership opportunity extended to all staff: committed to the 308b Cooperative structure. Real equity and profitshare.

Service Continuum

Different forms of hospitality,
the same local ingredients.

i.
Breakfast, Bakery & Morning Drinks
Coffee, tea, shakes. Yogurt and granola. Cottage cheese. Cranberry bars. Everyday entry point — accessible to every visitor.
iv.
Local Pizza
Flagship product, touching every menu. Hot and fresh, or take a frozen one home for later.
ii.
Grab-and-go & Walk-in Lunch
Counter service with seating. Bowls, salads, sandwiches, rotisserie chicken, breads and dips, charcuterie and cheese, picnic baskets and boxed lunches. Farmers market weekly with fresh produce and dairy daily.
v.
Dinner
Elegant, humble Minnesotan farm-to-table, individual plates and shared platters. Classic dinner service with high personal touch, including host, reservations, walk-ins, bar seating, servers, wine, cocktails, NA beverages, locally produced dishware. Great for families, for dates, for international guests.
iii.
Weekly Meal Prep
Derived from the farmer's market week to week. Breakfast, lunch, and dinner prepared and delivered to members weekly. Nutritionally focused comfort foods. Offered as grab and go.
vi.
Chef Menus & Patron Events
Tied to exhibitions, artists, cinema, theater, and other VIP moments: supporting Walker's event needs.

Product Expression

Sourdough crusts from freshly milled local grain. Mozzarella and brick cheese from Wisconsin dairy. Tomatoes from our markets, or our backyards, or Dei Fretelli from Ohio. Classic and seasonal toppings, just have a good time.

Local Pizza is an accessible, popular expression of our local-first mentality. We bring this local touch to every menu, every meal, every dish, every visitor.
