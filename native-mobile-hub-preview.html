Native iPhone and Android Community Hub

The subscriber portal becomes a mobile operating layer: current menu becomes Today, dish feedback becomes object feedback, note-to-chef becomes a private thread, linked accounts become gated roles, and past menus become history inside a member profile.

Lark: action in chat Todoist: Today + capture Cinny: spaces + threads

The product overlap

The current portal is already a gated relationship app. This version generalizes it from weekly meals into any recurring real-world group.

Subscriber portal becomes

This Week Today and upcoming schedule objects
Past Menus History of events, orders, feedback, attendance
Dish Feedback Feedback on dishes, events, resources, shifts
Note to Chef Object-bound private thread with staff routing
Linked Accounts Households, teams, vendors, customers, staff

Native design rules

1
Today is the home screen

No generic feed first. Lead with what changed, what is due, and where the user needs to be.

2
Spaces are gated groups

Customers, staff, vendors, and guests can share selected objects without sharing the whole workspace.

3
Threads attach to objects

A shift, event, resource, or order owns its discussion and task trail.

4
Tasks stay lightweight

Todoist-like capture, due dates, assigned work, and clear completion states.

Reference translation

Lark influence

Chat is not isolated. Messages can produce tasks, approvals, reminders, and schedule changes inside the same object.

Todoist influence

Everything actionable lands in Today, with fast capture, due dates, assigned work, filters, and completion.

Cinny influence

Spaces and rooms map cleanly to gated communities and object threads without pretending everything is one feed.
