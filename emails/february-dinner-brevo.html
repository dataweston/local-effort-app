The February Menu

Minnesota isn't exactly.. upbeat right now, is it? Most of my friends and family are pretty depressed, and I know we're not alone.

I learned in 2021 that I would be cooking until and through the fall of society if it ever comes. Maybe we're not there, but locally, no one knows what amount of 'living your life' is appropriate right now.

Live fully. This mid-winter menu is for our spirits and yours. Let us cook your family a hearty meal in the safety and comfort of your well-adorned home and kitchen.

Dinner in Your Home

We'll come over an hour before dinner time and prepare the meal in your kitchen. We'll use your pots and pans, and we'll bring anything specialized that we need. Your dinner price includes dishware and flatware, service, and cleanup when we're done. The experience is intended to feel like your home has transformed into a restaurant.
