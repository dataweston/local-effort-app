Local Effort Customer Update

Hello from Weston and Catherine!

A note for our current subscribing customers.

This email goes out to all current subscribing customers, you cool cats.

We are over the hump on a very tough transition: we moved homes from Roseville to St. Louis Park and kitchens from Hamline-Midway to Near North at the same time.

NEON Collective Kitchens is an incubator in the Northside for food entrepreneurs. Their new kitchen is remarkable, we’re really lucky and proud to be a part of it. We can confidently grow to any size at our pace.

Open House

There is a grand opening and open house celebration on Thursday, May 21 from 12-3pm. We’d love to see any of you, if you want to come check out what the big deal is.

View the Eventbrite

We know the last few weeks have been pretty bumpy, food-wise. It was important to us that we get through our move without canceling or skipping any production, and we’re proud that we accomplished this. That said, we don’t want to send the impression that we’re growing into something that doesn’t value the intimate, high quality service we’ve tried to embody.

In that spirit:

  • We’ve partnered with a new chef, Maria Beck, formerly a pastry chef at Hyacinth and a long list of other respected local outlets. We’re excited for the fresh energy on our menus, and looking forward to smoother production.
  • We’ve made a second acqui-hire on the administrative side, that we cannot disclose just yet. She’s not a replacement for texting the chef directly, but we look forward to elevating communication across all channels.
  • Most importantly, we are not adding new customers. It’s very important to us that your service is perfected in our new space, before we add to our work list.
  • Finally, we’re happy to officially announce that we are now a Worker’s Cooperative in the state of Minnesota. Under structure 308b, Local Effort Cooperative will offer high quality local food services in all settings from chefs and professionals who own the work. We have so much more information coming on this subject, but definitely do reach out if you have questions or insights.

This month continues the return of the farmers market and the real good season to hang with Local Effort. I’m continuing to build out the ordering and customer portal systems for meal prep families, which is a small technology problem and a larger set of logistics hurdles. Really grateful to be building a team and a community of people who care about high integrity food.

Til next time,
Weston
