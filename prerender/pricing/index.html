Personal chef pricing FAQ

Weekly meal prep plans begin around $275 per person per week for 15 meals. This is a median price based on typical variables. Pricing is always calculated for each client's actual needs, with discounts for couples, families, and longer-term billing.

How much does a personal chef cost in Minneapolis?

How much does a weekly meal plan cost?

Weekly meal prep plans begin around $275 per person per week for 15 meals. This is a median price based on typical variables. Pricing is always calculated for the client's actual needs and we discount for couples, families, and longer-term billing.

What is the cost for a small event or party?

A simple food drop-off service starts as low as $25 per person. Full-service coursed and plated meals typically range from $55 to $125 per guest, based on ingredients.

How much does an intimate dinner at home cost?

An intimate dinner at your home generally ranges from $95 to $135 per person depending on guest count, number of courses, and specialty sourcing.

How much is a private pizza party?

Private pizza parties start at $300 for groups up to 15 and scale with additional toppings, sides, travel distance, and service style.
